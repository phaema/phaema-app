[[letter_start]]

**{{ premises }}**
**Proposed lease: exclusion of security of tenure**

The council proposes to grant you a lease of the above premises for {{ proposed_term }} from {{ lease_start }}, on terms that sections 24 to 28 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 will not apply. This means you will not have a right to a new lease when it ends.

The law requires the council to give you a warning notice before you commit to the lease, and requires you to make a declaration that you have received it and accept its consequences. The warning notice is enclosed. Please read it carefully, and we recommend that you take independent professional advice.

- If you receive the notice at least 14 days before you sign the lease or become bound to take it, you need only sign the simple declaration.
- If not, the declaration must be a statutory declaration made before an independent solicitor or other person able to administer oaths.

Please return the signed declaration to the council's legal team, quoting our reference. The lease cannot be completed without it.

[[signature]]

# Internal checklist (delete before issue)

1. Use the current prescribed warning notice (Schedule 1 of the 2003 Order) and declaration (Schedule 2), unaltered.
2. Serve the warning notice on the tenant before they enter into the lease or become contractually bound to do so.
3. If served 14 or more days before: simple declaration. If fewer than 14 days: statutory declaration.
4. The lease must refer to the notice and declaration, and contain or be endorsed with the agreement to exclude.
5. Keep the notice, declaration and evidence of service on case {{ case_reference }}.
