I am pleased to confirm that the council, as landowner, gives permission for {{ activity }} at the above location on {{ dates }}, on the following conditions:

1. You will pay the council's fee of {{ fee_amount }} before the activity starts.
2. You will hold public liability insurance of at least £5 million for the activity and send us a copy of the certificate before it starts.
3. You will indemnify the council against any claim, loss or damage arising from the activity.
4. You will leave the land clean and in the same condition as before, and pay for any damage.
5. You will obtain any other permissions needed, such as planning permission, highway licences or premises licences.
