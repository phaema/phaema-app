## Internal notes (delete before service)

- A section 146 notice is not needed for forfeiture for non-payment of rent.
- Check the lease's forfeiture clause and that the council has not waived the right to forfeit (for example by demanding or accepting rent with knowledge of the breach).
- For repairing covenants: where the lease was granted for seven years or more and three or more years remain, the 1938 Act statement in paragraph 4 is required.
- Serve on the tenant and any guarantor, by a method the lease or section 196 of the Law of Property Act 1925 allows, and keep evidence of service on case {{ case_reference }}.
