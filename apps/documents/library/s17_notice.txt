## Notes for the officer

1. Serve within **six months** of each sum falling due, or the council loses the right to recover that sum from the former tenant or guarantor.
2. Use the current prescribed form, with its notes. Check whether the former tenant gave an authorised guarantee agreement, and whether the lease was granted before or after 1 January 1996.
