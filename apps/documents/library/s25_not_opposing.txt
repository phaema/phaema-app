## Before the notice is served

1. Check the tenancy is protected by Part 2 of the 1954 Act: current status {{ security_of_tenure }}.
2. Check the council is the "competent landlord" for the tenancy (section 44).
3. Check the termination date: not earlier than {{ term_end }}, and not less than 6 nor more than 12 months after the notice is given.
4. Name every tenant exactly as in the tenancy, as it now stands after any assignment.
5. Use the current prescribed Form 1, with all its notes, and have the council's legal team check it.
6. Serve by a method the lease or section 23 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1927 allows, and keep evidence of service.
7. Record the notice on case {{ case_reference }} in PHAEMA: the date given and the termination date. PHAEMA then shows the last day to apply to court.
