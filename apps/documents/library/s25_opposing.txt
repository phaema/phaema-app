## Before the notice is served

1. Check the tenancy is protected by Part 2 of the 1954 Act: current status {{ security_of_tenure }}.
2. Check the council is the "competent landlord" (section 44).
3. Check the termination date: not earlier than {{ term_end }}, and not less than 6 nor more than 12 months after the notice is given.
4. Confirm the decision to oppose, and the grounds, have been approved (case {{ case_reference }}). For ground (f) or (g), keep the evidence of the council's intention, such as a Cabinet or committee resolution and funding.
5. Consider whether compensation under section 37 will be payable (grounds (e), (f) and (g)).
6. Use the current prescribed Form 2, with all its notes, and have the council's legal team check it.
7. Serve by a method the lease or section 23 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1927 allows, and keep evidence of service.
8. Record the notice on the case in PHAEMA, so the court deadline is tracked.
