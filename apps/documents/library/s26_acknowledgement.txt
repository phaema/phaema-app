case_types: renewal, expiry
description: Acknowledges the tenant's request for a new tenancy, without committing the council, and sets out the next steps and dates.

[[letter_start]]

**{{ premises }}**
**Your request for a new tenancy under section 26 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954**

Thank you for your request for a new tenancy dated {{ notice_served_on }}, proposing that the new tenancy begins on {{ notice_date }}.

The council is considering your request. If the council intends to oppose the grant of a new tenancy, it will tell you within two months of your request, as the Act requires.

We would be happy to discuss terms for a new tenancy with you or your adviser. Any discussions will be subject to contract.

Please note that unless terms are agreed, or the time is extended by written agreement before it expires, an application to the court must be made before {{ notice_date }}. The council recommends that you take professional advice.

[[signature]]
