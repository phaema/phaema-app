**Assumptions and special assumptions.** Please agree any assumptions and special assumptions with us in advance and state them in your report.

**Components.** Where the building value exceeds the council's componentisation threshold, report the value and remaining useful life of each significant component, and the split between land and buildings.

**Report.** Your report should comply with the Red Book reporting standards, including a statement of any material valuation uncertainty.

**Use of the report.** The report is for the purpose above and may be disclosed to the council's auditor. It may not be relied on by others or published without your consent, except as the law or the council's accounts require.
