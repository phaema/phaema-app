# Glossary

1954 Act
:   The Landlord and Tenant Act 1954, Part II. Business tenants **inside** the Act have a right to renew their
    lease. A lease can be **contracted out** of the Act by following a set procedure before it is granted.

Agreement for lease
:   A contract to grant a lease in the future, often once building work is finished.

Apportionment
:   Charging part of a period's rent, by the day, when an agreement starts or ends part way through a period.
    See [raising rent](rent-ledger.md#raising-rent).

Arrears
:   Rent that is overdue: the due date has passed and it has not been paid or credited.

Break option
:   A right in a lease for the landlord, the tenant or either to end it early on a set date, usually by giving
    notice by a deadline. See [breaks](events.md#breaks).

Easement
:   A right over someone else's land, such as a right of way.

FRI (full repairing and insuring)
:   A lease where the tenant pays for all repairs and the cost of insuring the building.

GIA (gross internal area)
:   Floor area measured to the inside of the external walls, including walls, stairs and plant rooms. Measured
    under the RICS Code of Measuring Practice.

Golden Thread
:   The accurate, up-to-date information about a building that the Building Safety Act 2022 requires for
    higher-risk buildings, and good practice for all. PHAEMA will support it in a later module.

Holding over
:   A tenant staying in occupation after the lease's term end, often while a renewal is agreed.

INSPIRE Index Polygons
:   HM Land Registry's free boundaries of registered freehold titles. See [maps](maps.md#land-registry-inspire-boundaries).

Licence
:   Permission to use property that does not give the occupier a lease or security of tenure.

NIA (net internal area)
:   The usable floor area inside a building, excluding common parts, stairs, toilets and plant. Usually the area
    rent is based on for shops and offices.

Option to tax
:   A landlord's choice to charge VAT on rent from a commercial property, which would otherwise be exempt. In
    PHAEMA, agreements where the council has opted to tax are marked VAT chargeable.

Peppercorn rent
:   A nominal rent, often nothing at all, used where a lease is granted for community benefit or a premium.

Quarter days
:   The traditional English rent days: 25 March, 24 June, 29 September and 25 December.

Rent review
:   A point in a lease where the rent is reassessed, for example to open market value. "Upwards only" means the
    rent cannot go down. See [rent reviews](events.md#rent-reviews).

Rent roll
:   The total rent receivable from all current lettings.

Service charge
:   A tenant's share of the landlord's costs of shared services, paid on account against a budget and balanced
    against the actual costs after the year end. See [service charges](service-charges.md).

Void share
:   The share of service charge costs that falls on empty units, which the landlord pays.

Tenancy at will
:   A tenancy that either side can end at any time, often used for short-term occupation.

Terrier
:   A council's register of the land and property it owns.

UPRN (Unique Property Reference Number)
:   The national identifier for every addressable place in Great Britain, from the AddressBase and local land and
    property gazetteer. Using it lets PHAEMA link to GIS, council tax, business rates and other systems.

USRN (Unique Street Reference Number)
:   The national identifier for every street.

WAULT (weighted average unexpired lease term)
:   The average time left on the council's leases, weighted by rent, so bigger leases count for more. A common
    measure of how secure an income is. See [the dashboard](dashboard.md#rent-roll).

Wayleave
:   Permission, usually for a payment, for a utility company to run cables or pipes across land.
