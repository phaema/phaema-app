# Your buildings (for departments)

This page is for people in council departments who have been named by the estates team as their department's
**property liaison**, or as someone who **can see, read only**, such as the head of service.

## What you can see

Sign in as you do for other council systems. You go straight to **Your buildings**: every council building your
department (and any teams within it) uses, with the space you hold, the people based there and the desks or
places available.

Open a building to see:

- its address and your **estates contact**, the surveyor who manages it;
- the **stacking plan**: each floor, with every department and organisation in the building named;
- **your space and people**: your own floor space, your share of shared space such as reception and meeting
  rooms, the people based there, the capacity, and how much of the capacity is in use.

You see figures for your own department only, and nothing else in PHAEMA.

## Recording how many people are based here

If you are the property liaison, open a building and fill in **Record how many people are based here**:

- **People based in this building**: everyone whose usual place of work is here, whether or not they come in
  every day. A count only: never names.
- **Full-time equivalent**, if you have it.
- **As at**: the date the count applies to. It cannot be in the future.

The figure is used straight away. Your estates contact sees it on their dashboard, checks it, and may ask you
about it. Until then it shows as **Waiting to be checked**. Every figure records who entered it and when.

People who can see, read only, cannot record figures.

## If something is wrong

Floor areas, which spaces you hold and capacity are kept by the estates team. If any of them look wrong, contact
your estates contact. If you cannot see a building your department uses, the estates team has not recorded your
department there yet.

## For the estates team

In **Manage data**, **Occupiers**, choose the **property liaison** and anyone who **can see, read only** for each
department. They need a PHAEMA account but no role; with the space module switched on, naming them is what gives
them access. They see only the occupiers they are named on and those below them. Remove them from the occupier
to end their access.

When a liaison records headcount, the building's managing surveyor sees **Headcount from a department to check**
on their dashboard. Open it, compare it with the earlier figure, and select **Mark as checked**.
