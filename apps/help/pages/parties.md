# Parties

A party is anyone the council has an agreement with: a tenant, landlord, licensee or guarantor. Each party is
recorded once and linked to all their agreements.

## A party's page

Select **Leases**, then **Parties**, and choose a party to see:

- their contact details and company or charity number;
- every agreement where they are tenant, landlord or guarantor;
- every property involved;
- the total rent they pay or receive.

## Finance customer reference

Record the party's customer account number from the council's finance system. It links PHAEMA to the finance
system and is used to match parties when [importing](import-export.md#parties).

## Who can change parties

Administrators and surveyors can change contact details. Every change is in the [change log](change-log.md).
