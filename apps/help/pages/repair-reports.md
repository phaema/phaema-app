# Repair reports

Tenants can report repairs in the [tenant portal](tenant-portal.md), and council departments' property liaisons can
report them on their [buildings pages](occupier-contacts.md). Both use the same form: which building or unit, where
the problem is, what is wrong, whether anyone may be at risk, a phone number for access and up to 3 photos. Photos
are reduced in size and their location data is removed.

A report does not become a job by itself. It waits in the **triage queue** until someone in the estates team decides
what happens. This needs the [maintenance](maintenance.md) module to be on.

## Who sees new reports

The managing surveyor of the property sees **Repair report to look at** on their dashboard; administrators see
every report, including buildings no one manages. Reports marked as a possible risk say so and come first. The full
queue is **Maintenance → Repair reports**.

## Deciding

Open the report to see the details, the photos, who reported it and, for tenants, the agreement's repairing terms.
Then choose one:

- **Raise a job**: say what needs doing and choose the category and priority. PHAEMA works out who pays from the
  repairing terms, as for any job, and sets the target date from the priority. The job shows a link back to the
  report and its photos.
- **Already reported**: link it to an open job at the property.
- **The tenant's to repair under the agreement** (tenants only).
- **Close without a job**.

Write a **reply** for the person who reported it. A reply is needed for the last two choices. Only administrators
and surveyors can decide.

## What the person who reported it sees

On the agreement page in the tenant portal, or the building page for departments: each report with its progress
(waiting to be looked at, job raised, work ordered, work done, the tenant's to repair, already reported or closed),
the target date, who pays and your reply. Read-only people in a department can follow reports but not make them.

Everything is in the change log; reports from the portal are marked **Tenant portal** with the tenant's name.
