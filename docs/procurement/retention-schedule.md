# Retention schedule for PHAEMA records

Suggested periods, to compare with the council's own retention schedule and the
[Local Government Classification Scheme](https://irms.org.uk/page/LGCS) published by the Information and Records
Management Society. The council's records manager decides the periods. PHAEMA does not delete records
automatically yet: an administrator deletes them, and reports can list what has passed its date.

| Record | Suggested period | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property register entry (land and buildings owned) | Permanent while owned; 15 years after disposal | Title history and enquiries about former land |
| Leases, licences and deeds (signed documents) | 15 years after the agreement ends | Claims on a deed run for 12 years (Limitation Act 1980, s.8), plus time to bring them |
| Agreement record (terms, rent schedule, events) | 15 years after the agreement ends | Kept with the deed |
| Former tenants still liable (AGAs) and guarantors | Until the liability ends, then 6 years | Claims under the Landlord and Tenant (Covenants) Act 1995 |
| Rent accounts, charges, receipts, credit notes | Current year plus 6 years | Financial records and claims in simple contract (Limitation Act 1980, s.5); longer while a debt or payment plan is open |
| Rent deposit accounts | 6 years after the deposit is returned or used | Financial record |
| Service charge budgets, accounts and reconciliations | Current year plus 6 years | Financial record; tenants' rights to inspect |
| Cases (consents, renewals, dilapidations, enquiries) | 6 years after the case closes | Claims in contract |
| Cases leading to a disposal or acquisition | 15 years after completion | Kept with the property record |
| Valuations | Permanent for assets still held; 6 years after disposal | Accounts and audit |
| Maintenance jobs and works orders | Current year plus 6 years | Financial record and claims |
| Compliance certificates (gas, electrical, fire risk, asbestos, legionella) | Life of the building, or at least the period the regulations require | Building safety; the Golden Thread for higher-risk buildings is kept for the building's life |
| Site visit records and photos | 6 years after the case closes | Kept with the case |
| Projects | 15 years after completion for capital projects; 6 years otherwise | Construction claims run for 12 years under seal |
| Change log (audit trail) | 7 years | Evidence of who changed records; a database administrator removes older entries with the purge setting described in the technical manual |
| User accounts | Disabled on leaving; deleted 1 year later, keeping their change log entries without the name | Access control; accountability |
| Emails filed on cases | As the case | Kept with the case |
| Emails to tenants (PHAEMA's copy: demands, invitations, codes masked, reminders, reply notices) | Current year plus 6 years | Evidence of what was sent; kept with the rent account |
| Copies held by GOV.UK Notify | 7 days by default (set in the council's Notify settings) | Notify's own retention; nothing needs keeping there |
| Tenant portal accounts | Access stops by itself 12 months after the last agreement the tenant can see ends; then remove the account. PHAEMA does not yet remove or anonymise these accounts automatically: [say who does, and how often] | Access ends automatically; the change log keeps who did what |
| Sign-in codes | Deleted by the daily tasks the day after they are made | Kept only as a keyed hash; valid 15 minutes |
| Messages with tenants | As the agreement's correspondence: 6 years after the agreement ends, or with the case if about a request | Correspondence |
| Contact changes asked for by tenants | 6 years after the decision | Evidence of who changed billing details |
| Repair reports and photos | As maintenance jobs: current year plus 6 years | Kept with the job, or on their own if no job was raised |
| Certificates and insurance documents sent by tenants | As compliance certificates and lease documents above | Same records, sent a different way |
| Backups | 14 days on the server (the installer's default); off-site copies per the council's backup policy | Recovery |

These periods are a starting point, not legal advice.
